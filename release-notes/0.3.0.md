## Shorter prompts

The built-in prompts were essays — half a screen of "keep the author's voice, do not
touch the line breaks" per action, sent with every request. They are a few sentences now.
The part about markdown, wiki links and poems is written once and added to every rewriting
action, yours included, so your own prompt does not have to warn the model about `[[links]]`
by itself.

Prompts you rewrote stay yours. The factory ones are replaced by the new versions, and so
are the names of the actions that were renamed.

**"Clean up transcript" is gone.** If you never edited it, it is removed on load; if you
did, it stays as your own action.

## Quick menu: your own layout

- **Five keys to begin with, up to nine** — the rest you add yourself, when five is not
  enough. The empty tail of the row is not there taking up space anymore.
- **A row can be dragged by its handle.** The key numbers stay put; what is assigned to
  them moves. A layout is a thing your hand remembers, and it is easier to drag a row than
  to reassign four dropdowns in a chain.
- **The pencil works on an empty key too** — there it creates an action instead of opening
  one.
- **The settings screen no longer jumps back to the top** on every change: the list of keys
  redraws by itself.

## A fragment in the chat

Asking about a selection used to send it as the question, and the model answered with "so
what should I do with it?". Now the fragment comes to the panel as an attachment above the
input: you see what you are asking about, a cross drops it, and it goes with the question
— into the feed as a collapsed quote, into the context of the questions that follow, and
into the saved conversation note. So the next question can be just "and shorter?".
